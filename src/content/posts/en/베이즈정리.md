### **What is Bayes' Theorem?**
Bayes' theorem is a law of probability used to **infer the cause after an event has occurred**.  
In other words, it provides a way to **update the probability of another event occurring based on an event that has already occurred**.


---


## **1. Formula for Bayes' Theorem**
Bayes' theorem is expressed as follows using conditional probability:


![](/images/velog/2c06a7a0bd676b99.png)



- P(A | B): **The probability that A occurred after B occurred** (**Posterior Probability**)  
- P(B | A): **The probability that B occurs given that A has occurred** (**Likelihood**)  
- P(A): **The probability that A occurs** (**Prior Probability**)  
- P(B): **The probability that B occurs** (the probability that B occurs among all possible cases)  


---


## **2. Why Is Bayes' Theorem Important?**
Bayes' theorem is the process of **updating probabilities when new information is provided**.  
In other words, it **calculates the posterior probability by modifying the existing probability (prior probability) using new information (likelihood)**.


- **Medical diagnosis:** Update the probability of having a disease based on test results
- **Machine learning:** The process by which a model updates probabilities when new data is provided
- **Criminal investigation:** Calculate the probability that a suspect is the perpetrator based on the given evidence
