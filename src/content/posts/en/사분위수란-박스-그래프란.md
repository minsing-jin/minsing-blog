# What Is the Interquartile Range (IQR) & How to Interpret a Box Plot


![](/images/velog/e60a51f60c1efce7.png)


1. First, find the **median** of the data.
2. Find the **median** of each of [minimum | median] | [median | maximum].
3. Q1 and Q3 are the range of the **medians** of the ranges [minimum | median] | [median | maximum] from step 2.
4. The interquartile range is the difference between the medians of the two groups divided based on the **median**.
-> In other words, at least 50% of the data values are distributed between Q1 and Q3.
5. The whiskers on both sides of the box are the maximum values of the data.


**Median: ** The median is not the average; it is the value located in the middle of the number of data points. If the number of data points is even, the median is the average of the two middle numbers.


# Outliers (What Is an Outliar?)
- In a box plot, they are the values outside the whiskers, i.e., outside (Q1 - 1.5 * IQR) or (Q3 + 1.5 * IQR).
- When measuring a person's movements with something like an Apple Watch, outliers can be removed or utilized.
