I want to earn advertising revenue from a blog. However, expecting to make a lot of money at the beginning can lead you in the wrong direction.
Advertising revenue varies greatly depending on traffic, topic, time spent on the site, country, and ad rates.


The initial goal is not money but creating a site that can be approved.


## What You Need First


- Original articles
- About and contact pages
- Privacy policy
- A searchable structure
- A consistent publishing record


Without these elements, the site's credibility becomes a problem before advertising does.


## Criteria for Placing Ads


Ads should not interfere with the article. If readers come to read but see ads first, it is difficult to build a following. That is why this blog places ad slots at the bottom of articles and in natural positions.


First come the readers, and then comes monetization.
