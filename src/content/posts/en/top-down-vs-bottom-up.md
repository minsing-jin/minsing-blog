## minsing-sik One-Line Summary
#### 1. top-down
Look at a highly abstract concept and define the other conceptual elements by breaking it down step by step.


#### 2. bottom up
Start by understanding concrete component concepts, then assemble them to understand higher-level concepts.




## Top-Down
the top-down approach starts with a highly abstract concept. If we say that we are flushing a toilet, we begin the design with “pressing the button makes the water go down.” 
Press the button -> lever moves -> lever principle -> toilet flapper opens -> pre-filled water flows ....... This is the process of defining other specific design elements like these.


In other words, the top-down approach starts with something abstract and progressively increases the level of detail.



## Bottom-Up
This is an approach that starts with concrete elements, assembles them step by step, and works toward the general. You design the process as follows: fill the tank with water, open the flapper, use a lever to open the flapper, and press the toilet button to make the water go down.


In other words, concrete elements -> general concept
