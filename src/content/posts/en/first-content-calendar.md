Running a blog for a long time takes more than willpower. You need a rough
calendar for what to write and when.


## Weekly Structure


On Mondays, organize what you made the previous week. On Wednesdays, turn what you learned into a post.
On Fridays, reflect on problems where you failed or got stuck.


This way, even on days when you have no topic to write about, questions remain.


## Types of Posts


- Build log
- Retrospective
- Notes on what you learned
- Tools and workflows
- Product idea validation


The important thing is not to write every post perfectly. It is to build up a public
record at a steady rhythm.
