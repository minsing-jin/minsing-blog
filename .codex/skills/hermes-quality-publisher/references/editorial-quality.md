# Editorial and Ad Quality

## Publication standard

A publishable post must provide reader value independent of search traffic or ads.

- State the real problem, decision, experiment, or lesson.
- Include concrete firsthand evidence: what changed, why, how, and what happened.
- Distinguish observed facts from opinion or expectation.
- Remove repetition, generic motivation, inflated certainty, and keyword stuffing.
- Use descriptive headings that help scanning without turning every heading into a search phrase.
- Keep the author's tone. Edit for clarity, not for a generic corporate voice.
- Link related posts only when they add context.

Block publication when the post contains placeholders, unsupported factual claims, private data, copied passages without attribution, or mostly generic summary.

## Ad policy

Article prose and ad delivery must remain separate.

- Never place ad markup in Markdown or MDX content.
- Keep the default single post ad after the article and newsletter CTA.
- Keep the homepage ad after substantive post listings.
- Do not modify a headline, introduction, section order, or conclusion to create ad inventory.
- Do not add another ad slot unless real traffic and engagement data justify it and the user explicitly approves.
- Prefer fewer ads over lower completion rate, trust, newsletter conversion, or returning readership.

## Preview verdict

Use one verdict:

- `READY`: no blockers; warnings are immaterial.
- `REVISE`: valuable draft with specific fixable gaps.
- `BLOCK`: unsafe, private, copied, misleading, or too incomplete to publish.

Always explain the verdict with evidence from the draft.
